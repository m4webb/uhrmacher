# Encounter — Bellows Chamber

**Difficulty:** Hard (~2,100 adjusted XP for 8 level 3 PCs)
**Target duration:** 3 rounds

---

## Enemies

### Stirge (x6) — CR 1/8
| AC | HP | Speed | Attack | Damage |
|----|-----|-------|--------|--------|
| 13 | 5 | 10 ft, fly 40 ft | +5 to hit | 6 (1d6+3) piercing + attach |

**Attach:** While attached, cannot attack. Target takes 5 (2d4) necrotic at start of stirge's turn. Any creature within 5 ft can use an action to detach it.

### Swarm of Stirges (x2) — CR 2
| AC | HP | Speed | Attack | Damage |
|----|-----|-------|--------|--------|
| 14 | 36 | 10 ft, fly 40 ft | +5 to hit | 14 (2d10+3) piercing, or 8 (1d10+3) if bloodied |

**Resistances:** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
**Immunities:** Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned
**Swarm:** Can occupy another creature's space. Can move through any Tiny opening.
**Grapple:** Medium or smaller targets in the swarm's space are grappled (escape DC 13). Grappled targets take 7 (2d6) necrotic at end of their turn.

---

## Waves

| Wave | Trigger | Enemies |
|------|---------|---------|
| 1 — On entry | Party enters the chamber | 6 stirges (scattered on pipes and rafters) |
| 2 — First bellows | Players clear or open the first bellows | 1 swarm of stirges erupts from inside |
| 3 — Second bellows | Players clear or open the second bellows | 1 swarm of stirges erupts from inside |

---

## Environment

- **Two bellows** (one each side) — large wooden structures, climbable, provide half cover behind them
- **Brass pipes** — run up through ceiling, climbable but narrow
- **Maintenance alcove** — nook between one bellows and the wall (journal is here)
- **Debris and nesting material** — difficult terrain in patches around the bellows

---

## End Condition

All stirges dead or driven off. Both bellows must be cleared to restore the air channels.

---

## DM Notes

- Individual stirges go down in one hit — let players feel effective early
- Swarms are the real threat: B/P/S resistance means martial damage is halved, grapple + necrotic drain is dangerous
- Casters with AoE or magic damage shine against the swarms
- If the encounter is too easy, have a few surviving individual stirges re-emerge from crevices
- If too hard, a swarm at half HP scatters and flees up the pipes
