# Scene 5 — The Uhrmacher's Study

**Act:** II
**Type:** Social / Discovery
**Location:** Uhrmacher's Study (ground floor, hidden)

---

## Read-Aloud

*(If entering via hidden door:)*
The paneling swings inward to reveal a small room thick with dust. A writing desk cluttered with papers and an inkwell sits beneath bookshelves crammed with leather-bound volumes. Clock diagrams are pinned to the walls. Everything is untouched — cobwebs connect the chair to the desk. Someone left this room and never came back. And squatting on the desk — ignoring the chair entirely — is a young woman who absolutely does not belong here. Barefoot, twigs in her hair. She looks up with sharp, curious eyes. "And what are you here for?"

*(If entering via back window:)*
You pull yourself through the narrow window into a dusty, forgotten room. A clockmaker's study — desk, books, diagrams. No one has been here in years. Except for the young woman squatting on the desk, barefoot, with twigs in her hair. She tilts her head at you. "Well. You're not one of the little metal ones."

## NPCs

| NPC | Where | Disposition |
|-----|-------|-------------|
| **Moosfräulein Linde** (disguised) | Squatting on the desk, barefoot | Suspicious, curious, sizing them up. Can she use them? |

### Linde — Knows / Will Share / Won't Share

- **Knows:** The tower is killing the forest, the "little metal ones" are dangerous, the old clockmaker tried to stop it. She knows the document on the desk is important but can't read it — won't admit this.
- **Will share freely:** That the metal ones are dangerous, that the forest is dying because of the tower. She has a document she knows is important but won't hand it over yet.
- **Will share if she trusts the party:** She wants the tower destroyed. The clockmaker tried to stop it and failed. The modrons won't stop — she's tried sabotage and they just repair it. Force alone won't work. Hands over the contract. She'll help if they act against the modrons.
- **Won't share:** Her true form, her coven, her backup plan (attack with the coven if players side with modrons), that she can't read
- **Key line:** *"The old clockmaker understood, eventually. The question is whether you're faster learners than he was."*

---

## Secrets

- Linde is a green hag using Illusory Appearance — DC 20 Investigation to see through, or physical touch reveals bark-like skin
- If players side with the modrons or refuse to act against the tower, Linde leaves through the window. She returns in Act III with her coven.
- This is the branching point: desired path (stop the modrons) vs. alternate path (protect the modrons from the hag coven)

## Artifact — The Contract

The full Contract of Planar Accord (physical prop — `props/documents/contract.tex`). Linde has it and knows it matters, but can't read it. Players must earn her trust to get it. Art. VI §6.3 is the key: a failed activation sequence nullifies the contract entirely.
