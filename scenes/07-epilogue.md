# Epilogue — Beats

**Act:** III (aftermath)
**Type:** Roleplay — no script; run the beats that the table earned, in whatever order they chase them.

---

## Victory (cuckoo destroyed before three cycles)

1. **The tower stops.** The first true silence — no tick anywhere. Players holding the journal may recognize Wilhelm's line: *"The silence was immediate and complete. I had not realized how loud it was until it was gone."*
2. **The Gear.** If anyone investigates why the ticking stopped: in the winding room, the Cross-Planar Regulation Gear has **cracked cleanly in two** in its cradle. The contract is void, and the crystallized law knew it.
3. **The modrons go to Pendel.** Those on this plane stand down and gather to the highest-ranking modron present. What happens next depends entirely on what happened to Pendel — befriended/pushed rogue, frozen neutral, or antagonized (his sheet's branches). Play it out; there is no script for a rogue modron with a congregation.
4. **Fritz.** With the ticking gone, his leg stops working. Where he is depends on Act II: befriended, he watched the fight from the landing hatch; frightened or fought, he's below with the golem. Don't shortcut it — this is the cost the party chose. Let it land.
5. **Linde.** The tower is dead; she got what she came for. The forest recovers as it did after 1742 — mostly. The straight stretch of river stays straight.
6. **Freudenfeld.** Grüber pays 25gp a head on completion, delighted, understanding nothing. The conformity recedes beat by beat (run the escalation table backward). Eichenwald's classroom gets noisy again.

## Defeat (the tower finishes striking three)

- The planar anchor is permanent. The blessings of Order flow — roughly five leagues at full saturation per the contract's fine print, a radius "revised upward without notice."
- The modrons no longer need exterminators. The town thanks the party warmly, in increasingly similar voices.

## Alternate path (party defended the cuckoo)

- Same permanence, but chosen. The contract is fulfilled; the party is paid and remembered fondly by the Collective — a line item in a ledger on Mechanus.
- Linde's coven survives or doesn't; either way the Tiefenwald's wildness is on a clock.

---

## DM Notes

- The epilogue is where the Fritz dilemma pays off — everything else can be summarized, but beat 4 should be played.
- Pendel's fate is deliberately unresolved in his sheet; the epilogue is where the table resolves it.
