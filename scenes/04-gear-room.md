# Scene 4 — The Gear Room

**Act:** II
**Type:** Combat / Social
**Location:** Gear Room (below ground)

---

## Read-Aloud

Stone steps descend into a vaulted underground chamber. Heavy iron and brass gear trains line the walls, connected by thick axles and drive chains. In the center, a circular mounting cradle sits conspicuously empty — a gap in the machinery, like a missing tooth. Near the cradle, a thin bedroll, a wooden crate used as a table, a candle stub. Someone lives here. Then a massive shape steps out from behind the gears — a hulking figure of interlocking brass cogs and clock springs, amber eyes glowing. It puts itself between you and the camp.

---

## NPCs

| NPC | Where | Disposition |
|-----|-------|-------------|
| **Fritz** | Hiding behind the gear golem / behind machinery | Scared, defiant, skeptical of everyone |
| **Gear Golem** (flesh golem statblock) | Blocking the mounting cradle | Hostile — attacks on Fritz's standing orders to protect him |

### Fritz — Knows / Will Share / Won't Share

- **Knows:** He's been living here since his leg started working again. The tower makes him better. The golem protects him.
- **Will share if calmed:** He's scared of strangers. His leg was useless before. He doesn't want anyone to take the tower away from him.
- **Won't share (doesn't know):** What the Gear does, what the modrons are planning, the contract, the long-term consequences
- **Key line:** *"I could barely walk before. Now I can run. You're not taking that from me."*

---

## Secrets

- Fritz is hiding — players who look for the source of the golem's commands (Perception DC 12) can spot him behind the machinery
- Fritz does not know the Gear needs to be installed here — players can persuade him it will help the tower (Persuasion DC 12) or deceive him about their intentions
- If players tell Fritz they want to harm the tower, he orders the golem to fight to the death
- The gear receipt (packing manifest) is filed near the mounting cradle

---

## Artifact — Gear Receipt

The original packing manifest from the Modron Collective (contract Art. II §2.1). Modron bureaucratic language, Mechanus serial numbers, planar specifications. First concrete evidence the tower is connected to another plane. References an installation manual — enough detail for players to attempt installing the Gear themselves without Pendel.

## Transition

Gear is installed in the mounting cradle. The tower mechanism engages — a deep vibration, gears meshing, the sound of the clock accelerating. The activation sequence has begun. Players need to get to the apex.
