# Trinkets — Unique Treasures

Ten items, placed by the DM whenever exploration earns them. Player handouts:
`render/handouts/trinkets.pdf` (one page each, image frames wired to
`images/trinket-*.png` — re-export after generating images).

| Item | Effect | Natural spot |
|------|--------|--------------|
| **Die Stimmgabel** (Foreman's Fork) | Action — strike: one modron of tridrone rank or below within 60 ft that hears it treats you as its superior; it obeys your directive on its next turn | Clockhall, among the modron work orders |
| **The Unpunctual Watch** | Once, when you or an ally within 30 ft makes a d20 roll: click the crown, the roll is made again, new result stands. Stops until wound on a long rest | Study desk drawer |
| **Uhrmacheröl, Grade 3** | 1 dose. Bonus action — slick a weapon: 1 minute, advantage + extra 1d6 damage vs constructs | Bellows workbench |
| **Das Küken** (the Chick) | Action — wind and release: 1 minute, flies where directed (fly 40 ft), you perceive through its eyes and ears. AC 12, 1 HP; rewind with an action | Bellows crate area or study shelf |
| **Die Sprungfeder** (Mainspring Step) | Bonus action — release: this turn jump distance ×3 and +10 ft speed; then spent, anyone rewinds with an action | Winding room machinery |
| **Der Moosbund** (the Moss-Knot) | Once: when you would drop to 0 HP, drop to 1 instead; the moss crumbles | Clockhall spine, where the monodrones' brushes missed |
| **Der Minutenzeiger** (the Minute Hand) | +1 dagger; on a hit, target can't take reactions until the start of its next turn | Study desk — his letter opener |
| **Das Feinwerkzeug** (masterwork tools) | Pure loot — 50 gp | Clockhall workbench |
| **Kirschwasser, 1741** | Pure loot — 30 gp | Clockhall, the living corner by the hearth |
| **Die Schnitzfiguren** (carved figures) | Pure loot — 20 gp; players may recognize young Heinrich, Eichenwald, Grüber | Study shelf |
