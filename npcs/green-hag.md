# Moosweibchen (Moss Women) — Fey Antagonists

- **Folklore basis:** Holzfräulein / Moosweibchen from Black Forest folklore. Lives are bound to trees; bodies and clothing covered in moss. Ambiguous figures — plague-bringers but also healers who teach herbal medicine.
- **Statblock:** Green hag coven (3 total), reskinned as moss women
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral (not evil — oppose the modrons on principle, not malice)
- **Lead hag disguised form:** Young woman of the forest, fairy-like. Clearly not from the village.
- **Disguise mechanic:** Illusory Appearance (Medium humanoid female only, visual only — touch or Investigation check reveals)
- **Found in:** Uhrmacher's Study (Act II) — only the lead hag, disguised
- **Motivation:** The modrons' lawful influence is killing the wild forest. Order drives the moss women out. They want to break the clock tower to preserve the wildness.
- **If players side with modrons:** Lead hag leaves in a huff, returns in Act III with her coven (3 moss women) + fey minions
- **Lead moss woman:** Moosfräulein Linde
- **Coven:** Moosfräulein Elke and Moosfräulein Briar; appear only if players side with modrons in Act III

Sharp, curious eyes with a slightly unsettling intensity — something ancient behind a youthful face. An amused, crooked half-smile. ### Linde — True Form (Moss Woman)
> An ancient creature of the deep forest. A hunched female figure whose skin is rough bark, draped in living moss and lichen. Long tangled hair of hanging moss and dead leaves. Eyes glow faintly green from deep sockets in a gnarled face. Fingers like twisted roots. Small mushrooms and ferns sprout from her shoulders and arms. She is unsettling but not monstrous — more ancient and wild than evil. Background of dense, dark Black Forest pines. [Append meta-prompt from portrait-style-guide.md]
