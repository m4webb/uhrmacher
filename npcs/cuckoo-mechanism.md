# The Cuckoo — Mechanus Construct

- **Type:** Large construct, lawful neutral
- **Role:** Act III boss — objective/HP sponge with dangerous defensive abilities
- **Found in:** Cuckoo's Landing (apex of the Kuckucksuhrturm)
- **Origin:** Dispatched from Mechanus through the planar aperture; not built in the tower
- **Restrained on the rod** — advantage on attacks against it, its attacks at disadvantage
- **Emerges on on-rounds (1, 3, 5), retracts on off-rounds (2, 4)**
- **Victory:** Destroy before it completes 3 full cycles (emerge, call, retract). A destroyed cuckoo cannot retract — cycle incomplete, contract null and void.

## Statblock

### Actions

**The Cuckoo's Call.** The cuckoo releases a resonant blast calibrated to the frequencies of Mechanus (659.7 Hz descending to 580.3 Hz). 30-foot cone, DC 14 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save: 3d8 thunder damage + poisoned until the end of the creature's next turn (flavored as ringing ears/disorientation — cancels the advantage granted by restrained). On a successful save: half damage, no poisoned. Fires once per cycle when the cuckoo calls.

### Reactions

**Emergency Venting.** Triggers once when the cuckoo drops below half HP (55). Superheated steam bursts from its joints. 10-foot radius, DC 14 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save: 3d6 fire damage. Half damage on a successful save.

### DM Options

**Defensive Lockdown.** At DM discretion, if the cuckoo is going down too fast: plating shifts, AC increases by 2 (to 18). Use to add tension, not as a default.
