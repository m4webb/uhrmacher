# Modron Overseer — Quadrone

- **Type:** Quadrone (CR 1, INT 10)
- **Role:** Social encounter NPC, not a combat threat
- **Found in:** Clock Tower First Room (Act II)
- **Purpose:** Manages the tower operation, directs lesser modrons (monodrones/duodrones)
- **Represents the lawful/modron side of the conflict to the players
- **On the verge of going rogue** — showing early signs of individuality
- Players may be able to befriend it and push it fully rogue, especially on the desired path (stopping the modrons)
- **Name:** Pendel (German for "pendulum" — chosen by itself, reflecting its swing between loyalty and individuality)
