# Heinrich — Old Woodcutter

- **Role:** Minor NPC, Freudenfeld local
- **Background:** Elderly woodcutter who has worked the Tiefenwald for decades
- **Found in:** The Gasthaus (Act I) — sitting in a corner, happy to tell stories to anyone who'll listen
- **Purpose:** Provides folklore context about the Tickwichtel. As a boy he saw them working in the tower — small mechanical creatures, clicking and whirring, very orderly. The old stories say they keep the tower running. He's delighted they're back. Treats it all as charming local legend, not a threat.
- **Tone:** Nostalgic, warm, a little rambly. The opposite of Eichenwald's anxiety.
