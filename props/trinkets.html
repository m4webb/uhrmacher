Die Stimmgabel
the Foreman's Fork
Action — strike the fork. One modron of tridrone rank or below within 60 feet that can hear it treats you as its superior: it obeys your directive on its next turn.
The Unpunctual Watch
it keeps its own time
Once, when you or an ally within 30 feet makes a d20 roll, click the crown: the last second unhappens — the roll is made again, and the new result stands. Stops until wound during a long rest.
Uhrmacheröl
clockmaker's oil
One dose. Bonus action — slick a weapon. For 1 minute, attacks with it against constructs have advantage and deal an extra 1d6 damage.
Das Küken
the Chick
Action — wind and release. For 1 minute it flies where you direct it (fly 40 ft) and you perceive through its eyes and ears. AC 12, 1 HP. Anyone may rewind it with an action.
Die Sprungfeder
the Mainspring Step
Bonus action — release the spring. This turn your jump distance is tripled and your speed increases by 10 feet. Then spent; anyone may rewind it with an action.
Der Moosbund
the Moss-Knot
Once, when you would drop to 0 hit points, you drop to 1 instead. The moss browns and crumbles to nothing.
Der Minutenzeiger
the Minute Hand
+1 dagger. On a hit, the target loses its next half-second: it can't take reactions until the start of its next turn.
Kirschwasser, 1741
cherry brandy, last of a good year
A wax-sealed bottle laid down the year before everything changed. 30 gp — more to someone who knows what happened after.
Das Feinwerkzeug

carved linden-wood figures

Die Doppelpfeife
the cuckoo's voice
Two silver-throated pipes drawn from deep in the wreck, tuned a breath apart — the Call, twice, waiting for air. An instrument maker would clear a shelf for them. 150 gp.

Hinged flight plates of otherworldly brass, each one identical to the last, none of them tarnished — nor ever will be. 100 gp.
