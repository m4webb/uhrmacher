Die Stimmgabel
the Foreman's Fork
Brass, tuned to a plane. Stamped along the handle: Ω-CVX-A11, PROPERTY OF THE COLLECTIVE — RETURN UPON COMPLETION OF WORKS. The works were never completed.
Action — strike the fork. One modron of tridrone rank or below within 60 feet that can hear it treats you as its superior: it obeys your directive on its next turn.
The Unpunctual Watch
it keeps its own time
It has never agreed with the tower — not once in sixty years. Inside the case, in a fine old hand: some seconds are worth disagreeing about.
Once, when you or an ally within 30 feet makes a d20 roll, click the crown: the last second unhappens — the roll is made again, and the new result stands. The watch then stops until wound during a long rest.
Uhrmacheröl
of no less than Grade 3 purity
The label insists, twice, on "no less than Grade 3 purity." This is Grade 3. It smells of brass and winter, and it knows where machines come apart.
Three doses. Bonus action — slick a weapon. For 1 minute, attacks with it against constructs have advantage and deal an extra 1d6 damage.
Das Küken
the Chick
A first try at a cuckoo — hand-carved, spring-driven, small enough to sit in a palm, and absurdly earnest about its work.
Action — wind and release. For 1 minute it flies where you direct it (fly 40 ft) and you perceive through its eyes and ears. AC 12, 1 HP. Anyone may rewind it with an action.
Die Sprungfeder
the Mainspring Step
The product of a sillier evening in the workshop. The housing fits a boot heel; the mainspring fits nowhere else in this world. Wind it, and mind the ceiling.
Bonus action — release the spring. This turn your jump distance is tripled and your speed increases by 10 feet. The spring is then spent; anyone may rewind it with an action.
