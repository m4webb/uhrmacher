Public Notice — Township of Freudenfeld
Pest Control Wanted

The township of Freudenfeld has need of a company of arms for the clearance of vermin from a structure in the Tiefenwald. Lodging and provisions for the evening prior furnished at the township's expense. Particulars upon presentation to the Bürgermeister.

25 Gold Pieces
per head, payable upon completion
Gottfried Grüber
Bürgermeister of Freudenfeld
