14. Oktobris, im Jahre des Herrn 1742

Something is wrong with the children.

They do not quarrel. They do not laugh. At the schoolhouse they sit in perfect rows and recite their lessons without error, without pause, without joy. Frau Eichenwald tells me she has not had to discipline a single child in three weeks. She says this not with pride but with fear.

Little Annaliese, who used to draw flowers on every surface she could find, now sits with her hands folded. When I asked her to draw me something, she looked at me as though I had spoken in a foreign tongue.

I built this tower to keep perfect time. I did not mean to keep it at such a cost.

— W.Z.
