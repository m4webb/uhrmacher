Caveat
14. Oktobris, 1742

Something is wrong with the children.

They do not quarrel. They do not laugh. At the schoolhouse they sit in perfect rows and recite their lessons without error, without pause, without joy.

I built this tower to keep perfect time. I did not mean to keep it at such a cost.

— W.Z.
