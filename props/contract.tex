{\large\bfseries\scshape\centering Recitals\par}

\textbf{WHEREAS}, the Uhrmacher has devoted his life and craft to the
pursuit of horological perfection, and desires above all temporal
instruments a mechanism of absolute and undeviating precision;

\textbf{WHEREAS}, the Collective possesses within its dominion certain
Apparatus of Planar Regulation, including gears forged in the
Eternal Clockwork of Mechanus, capable of synchronizing any mechanism
to the Pulse of Perfect Order;

\textbf{WHEREAS}, the Collective desires the establishment of a Planar
Anchor upon the Prime Material Plane for purposes of extending
beneficial order and the harmonious regulation of entropic variance;

\textbf{WHEREAS}, both parties recognize mutual advantage in the
arrangement herein described and enter into this Contract freely,
without duress, coercion, or undue influence\footnote{``Freely''
as defined in Mechanus Statute 44.7.2: the absence of external
force exceeding 12 Newtons of metaphysical pressure per unit of
will. Subjective feelings of reluctance, second thoughts, or
foreboding do not constitute duress under this definition.};

\textbf{NOW, THEREFORE}, in consideration of the mutual promises and
covenants herein contained, and for other good and valuable
consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby
acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

% ============================================================
%  ARTICLE I — DEFINITIONS
% ============================================================

\section{Definitions}

For the purposes of this Contract, the following terms shall have
the meanings ascribed to them below, and no other meanings, regardless
of common usage, colloquial understanding, or poetic license:

\subsection{The Tower}
``The Tower'' shall mean the structure known as the Kuckucksuhrturm,
situated in the Tiefenwald, including all floors, rooms, chambers,
sub-chambers, mechanical apparatus, decorative elements, and the
airspace extending vertically above the Tower to a height of no less
than forty (40) cubits, together with any subterranean vaults,
foundations, or passages connected thereto.

\subsection{The Gear}
``The Gear'' shall mean one (1) Cross-Planar Regulation Gear, forged
in the Foundries of Regulus, Mechanus, measuring precisely 2.7 cubits
in diameter, 0.4 cubits in thickness, composed of an alloy of
Mechanus-grade adamantine and crystallized law, serial designation
$\Omega$-CVX-0091\footnote{The Gear is the sole property of the
Collective. The Uhrmacher is granted a perpetual license of use,
not ownership. Unauthorized modification, defacement, or aesthetic
embellishment of the Gear constitutes a breach of this Contract and
an offense against Mechanus Statute 12.1.1 (Vandalism of Cosmic
Apparatus).}.

\subsection{Operational}
``Operational'' shall mean continuously functioning without interruption
for a period exceeding three (3) consecutive heartbeats of Mechanus,
as measured by a calibrated Modron chronometer, such that all gears,
springs, escapements, and ancillary mechanisms maintain synchronized
rotational output within a tolerance of $\pm 0.0001$ arc-seconds per
cycle.

\subsection{Activation}
``Activation'' shall mean the completion of the Planar Anchor Sequence
as described in Article IV, resulting in a stable and permanent
connection between the Tower and the Plane of Mechanus, through which
the blessings of Order may flow\footnote{``Blessings of Order'' is a
term of art referring to the progressive alignment of local reality
with Mechanus-standard parameters, including but not limited to:
regularization of biological variance, normalization of behavioral
patterns, optimization of structural geometry, and the gradual
elimination of entropic inefficiency. Side effects may include loss of
idiosyncrasy.}.

\subsection{The Uhrmacher}
``The Uhrmacher'' shall mean Wilhelm Zeiger, and shall further
encompass his heirs, successors, assigns, apprentices, or any
designated agents of contractual fulfillment acting under his
authority or assuming his obligations by operation of this Contract.

\subsection{Designated Agent of Contractual Fulfillment}
A ``Designated Agent of Contractual Fulfillment'' shall mean any
party who, by explicit assignment, implied succession, or operation of
Article V, Section 5.3, assumes the duties and obligations of the
Uhrmacher under this Contract\footnote{The determination of whether a
party qualifies as a Designated Agent shall be made solely by the
Collective, in its capacity as the party of superior organizational
competence. This determination is final, binding, and not subject to
appeal, arbitration, or dramatic protest.}.

% ============================================================
%  ARTICLE II — OBLIGATIONS OF THE COLLECTIVE
% ============================================================

\section{Obligations of the Modron Collective}

\subsection{Provision of the Gear}
The Collective shall deliver the Gear to the Tower within fourteen
(14) standard days of the execution of this Contract. Delivery shall
be accomplished via Planar Courier (Tridrone-class or above) and shall
include a packing manifest in triplicate.

\subsection{Technical Consultation}
The Collective shall provide, at no additional cost, the services of a
Quadrone-class technical advisor for a period not to exceed ninety (90)
standard days following delivery, to assist the Uhrmacher in the
installation and calibration of the Gear within the Tower's existing
mechanism\footnote{The technical advisor operates under Modron
Directive 9.3.1 and is not authorized to engage in small talk,
speculation, humor, or existential inquiry. Attempts to engage the
advisor in such activities shall not be considered a failure of
service.}.

\subsection{Planar Harmonics}
Upon successful Activation, the Collective shall maintain a stable
harmonic resonance between the Gear and the Eternal Clockwork of
Mechanus, ensuring the Tower keeps time with absolute precision in
perpetuity, or until the heat death of the local multiverse, whichever
occurs first.

% ============================================================
%  ARTICLE III — OBLIGATIONS OF THE CLOCKMAKER
% ============================================================

\section{Obligations of the Uhrmacher}

\subsection{Installation}
The Uhrmacher shall install the Gear into the central mechanism of
the Tower within sixty (60) standard days of delivery, integrating it
as the primary regulating element of the Tower's horological system.

\subsection{Maintenance}
The Uhrmacher shall maintain the Tower and all its mechanisms in
Operational condition at all times. Maintenance shall include, but not
be limited to: lubrication of all moving parts with clock oil of
no less than Grade 3 purity, replacement of worn components, removal
of debris, and the prevention of nesting by birds, bats, or other
fauna\footnote{The Collective has observed that Prime Material fauna
exhibit a regrettable tendency to roost in mechanical apparatus. The
Uhrmacher accepts full liability for any disruption caused by
biological interference, including but not limited to: bird nests in
gear trains, bat colonies in bell chambers, and spider webs across
escapement forks.}.

\subsection{Preparation for Activation}
The Uhrmacher shall ensure that the Tower is prepared for the
Activation Sequence as described in Article IV, including the
clearing and alignment of the Planar Aperture Chamber to receive
the Cuckoo Mechanism, the synchronization of the Tower's main
oscillator to the frequency of Mechanus, and the winding of the
Main Spring to full tension no less than one (1) hour prior to the
commencement of the ritual.

\subsection{Non-Interference}
The Uhrmacher shall not interfere with, obstruct, delay, or otherwise
impede the process of Activation once preparations have begun,
nor shall he disconnect, disable, or otherwise render non-Operational
any component of the Tower necessary for the completion thereof.

% ============================================================
%  ARTICLE IV — CONSUMMATION RITUAL
% ============================================================

\section{The Activation Sequence}

\subsection{Mechanism}
Activation shall be achieved by means of the Cuckoo Mechanism, a
device of no less than seven (7) cubits in height, maintained by the Collective on
the Plane of Mechanus (specifications filed as Appendix C with the
Kolyarut Registry). When the Tower's oscillator achieves synchronization
with the frequency of Mechanus, the Planar Aperture at the apex of
the Tower shall open, permitting the Cuckoo Mechanism to pass through
from Mechanus and emerge outward from the Tower\footnote{The
Cuckoo Mechanism is equipped with autonomous defensive systems
necessary for its operation in uncontrolled planar environments.
The Collective accepts no liability for incidental harm, structural
damage, or loss of life resulting from proximity to the Cuckoo
Mechanism during the Activation Sequence.}.

\subsection{The Three Cycles}
The Cuckoo Mechanism must complete three (3) full cycles of passage
through the Planar Aperture. Each cycle shall consist of:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item The opening of the Planar Aperture and full extension of the
Cuckoo Mechanism from the Plane of Mechanus outward through the Tower;
\item The sounding of the Cuckoo's Call---a two-tone harmonic sequence
calibrated to the frequency of Mechanus ($\nu_1 = 659.7$ Hz,
$\nu_2 = 580.3$ Hz);
\item Full retraction of the Cuckoo Mechanism back through the Planar
Aperture to the Plane of Mechanus.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Completion}
Upon the successful completion of the third cycle, the Planar Anchor
shall be deemed established, and the connection between the Tower and
Mechanus shall become permanent and irrevocable. The blessings of
Order shall thereafter flow continuously through the Anchor into the
surrounding region\footnote{The radius of effect shall expand at a
rate determined by the Collective's Calibration Office and is not
subject to negotiation. Initial estimates suggest a radius of
approximately five (5) leagues at full saturation, though this
figure may be revised upward without notice.}.

% ============================================================
%  ARTICLE V — TERM, CONTINUITY, AND SUCCESSION
% ============================================================

\section{Term, Continuity, and Succession}

\subsection{Duration}
This Contract shall remain in full force and effect from the date of
execution until such time as Activation is achieved, or until the
Contract is terminated in accordance with Article VI. There is no expiration date.

\subsection{Continuity of Obligation}
The obligations of the Uhrmacher under this Contract are attached to
the role, not merely to the person. Should the Uhrmacher become
unable or unwilling to fulfill his duties---whether by reason of
death, incapacitation, voluntary departure, involuntary departure,
existential crisis, or transformation into a non-corporeal
state---the obligations herein shall not lapse, but shall transfer
as provided in Section 5.3.

\subsection{Succession and Transfer}
In the event that the Uhrmacher fails to designate a successor prior
to the cessation of his stewardship, and has not executed a valid
termination under Article VI, stewardship of the Tower and all
obligations under this Contract shall transfer automatically to the
Modron Collective, acting in its capacity as Designated Agent of
Contractual Fulfillment and custodian of unfinished Order. The
Collective shall thereupon assume all rights, duties, and privileges
of the Uhrmacher for the sole purpose of bringing the Contract to
Activation\footnote{This clause exists purely as a contingency to
prevent contractual orphanhood and ensure the orderly completion of
mutual obligations. The Collective has no interest in acquiring
stewardship and would vastly prefer the Uhrmacher fulfill his duties
personally. This footnote is provided in the spirit of transparency
and should not be interpreted as evidence of premeditation.}.

\subsection{Resumption of Operations}
Upon assuming stewardship under Section 5.3, the Collective is
authorized to enter the Tower, restore all mechanisms to Operational
condition, complete any remaining preparations, and execute the
Activation Sequence without further consent, approval, or
authorization from any party, living or deceased.

% ============================================================
%  ARTICLE VI — TERMINATION
% ============================================================

\section{Termination}

\subsection{Termination by Mutual Consent}
This Contract may be terminated at any time by the written and
notarized consent of both parties, delivered simultaneously to the
Kolyarut Registry of Accords, Mechanus, in person and in
triplicate\footnote{``In person'' means physical presence on the
Plane of Mechanus. Astral projection, sending spells, and strongly
worded letters do not satisfy this requirement.}.

\subsection{Termination by Judicial Decree}
This Contract may be terminated by formal decree of the Kolyarut
Courts of Mechanus, upon petition by either party, provided that the
petitioner demonstrates a Material Breach of no fewer than three (3)
distinct contractual obligations by the opposing party, submits
evidence notarized by a Kolyarut Enforcer, and appears before the
Court in person on the Plane of Mechanus for a hearing scheduled no
sooner than one (1) Mechanus Standard Year from the date of
filing\footnote{One Mechanus Standard Year is equivalent to
approximately 365,242 Prime Material days, owing to the difference
in planar temporal density. Petitioners are advised to plan
accordingly.}.

\subsection{Termination by Failed Activation}
Should the Activation Sequence, once commenced, fail to reach
completion---whether by disruption of the Planar Aperture, forcible
interruption of any cycle, mechanical failure, or any other cause
preventing the completion of three (3)
full cycles---the Sequence shall be deemed irrevocably failed,
the Planar Anchor shall not be established, and this Contract shall
thereupon become null and void in its entirety, releasing
both parties from all obligations herein\footnote{The Collective
regards this clause as a mathematical formality. The probability of
ritual failure, given adequate preparation, is calculated at
0.00017\%. This figure accounts for all known variables and
several unknown ones.}.

\subsection{Termination by Unanimous Resolution}
This Contract may be terminated by unanimous resolution of the
Greater Modron Hierarchy, requiring the individual, written, and
notarized consent of every Modron from Monodrone to Primus---currently
numbering approximately thirty-six million---filed separately with the
Kolyarut Registry. A single abstention, dissent, or failure to file
shall render the resolution void.

\subsection{Effect of Disconnection Without Termination}
The mere disconnection of the Gear, the cessation of Tower operations,
or the abandonment of the Tower by the Uhrmacher shall not
constitute a valid termination of this Contract. Such actions shall be
deemed a temporary suspension of performance, and the obligations of
the Uhrmacher (or his successors, as determined under Article V)
shall resume upon the restoration of Operational conditions.
