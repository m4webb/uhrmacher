# The Uhrmacher's Study

- **Position:** Ground floor, hidden (off the Clockhall)
- **Clock anatomy:** None — personal space, not part of the mechanism
- **Encounter:** Social (Moosfraulein Linde, disguised)
- **Artifact:** The Contract

## Description

A small, dusty room untouched since the Uhrmacher's death. Cluttered writing desk, bookshelves with horological texts and personal volumes, diagrams of the tower mechanism pinned to the walls. A window in the back wall looks out onto the forest behind the tower. The room feels frozen in time — a half-finished cup of something on the desk, a pen left mid-sentence. The modrons have never entered; nothing in here is mechanically relevant to them. Linde (the moss woman, disguised) is here when the players find it.

## Traversal

- **Interior:** Hidden door from the Clockhall (concealed in wood paneling)
- **Exterior:** Window on the back of the tower (alternate entry for players who scout outside — climb up and in)

## Artifact — The Contract

The full Contract of Planar Accord (physical prop — `props/documents/contract.tex`). The most critical document in the adventure. Reveals the legal framework, the activation sequence, the termination clauses. Art. VI §6.3 is the key — a failed activation sequence nullifies the contract entirely.
