# Cuckoo's Landing

- **Position:** Apex of the tower
- **Clock anatomy:** Cuckoo door — the planar aperture
- **Encounter:** Boss (Cuckoo + escalating modron waves)
- **Artifact:** None (boss room)

## Description

The top of the tower, open and exposed. The floor is a single massive gear (25 ft radius) that rotates during the fight. On one wall, the planar aperture — a circular opening where the boundary between planes thins. The rod mechanism extends from the aperture inward; the cuckoo emerges along it. Air pipes from the bellows below terminate here, feeding into the cuckoo mechanism to power its call. When the activation sequence begins, the aperture glows with the cold, geometric light of Mechanus visible beyond.

## Traversal

- **Down:** Narrow passage alongside the air pipes, descending to the Bellows Chamber
