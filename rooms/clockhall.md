# The Clockhall

- **Position:** Ground floor, front door
- **Clock anatomy:** The Movement — mechanical heart of the tower
- **Encounter:** Social (Pendel)
- **Artifact:** Letter to a friend (optimistic view of the tower)

## Description

The front door opens directly into the main room of the tower. A living space and workshop fused together — the Uhrmacher both lived and worked here. A workbench, tools, a cold hearth, and simple furniture share the space with the exposed clock mechanism: gear trains, shafts, and the main oscillator running through the room like the tower's spine. Modron work crews (monodrones, duodrones) busy themselves maintaining the mechanism. Pendel oversees from a central position.

## Traversal

- **In:** Front door (main entry)
- **Up:** Staircase to the Bellows Chamber
- **Down:** Trapdoor/stairs to the Winding Room
- **Side:** Hidden door to the Uhrmacher's Study (concealed in the wood paneling — the modrons have never investigated it, not mechanically relevant to them)

## Artifact — Letter to a Friend

A letter on the Uhrmacher's workbench, partially buried under tools and modron work orders. Written early in the tower's operation, full of pride and optimism. The tower keeps perfect time, the town is thriving, the gear from Mechanus is a marvel. No hint of doubt yet.
