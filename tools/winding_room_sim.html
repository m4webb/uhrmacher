WINDING ROOM — COUNTERWEIGHTS (v3, six weights)

Six massive cast-iron PINECONE counterweights (3×3 squares) hang from winding chains, one above each berth (E2/E5/E8 upper, I2/I5/I8 lower). At any moment exactly two per level are down — four pinecones on the map, never more. Each pull-chain transfers load between two berths: one weight descends, another rises. A pull whose descending berth is already down grinds. Pulling costs an action.

PULL-CHAINS (party — 1 action each)

Chain labels: ↓berth = where a weight descends (that lane closes); the paired berth's weight rises (that lane opens). Blue shading = squares reachable from the entrance; a chain someone can't reach can't be pulled.
Fritz has no control panel — but the C8 chain hangs beside his corner. He can pull it himself: sealing himself in, and revealing the way to the cradle.
