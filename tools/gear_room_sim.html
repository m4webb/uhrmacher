GEAR ROOM — SLIDING GEARS (v2)

Four sliding gears at two levels. Each sits between lanes and always blocks one lane or the middle. The middle slot is shared — if it's occupied, the other gear cannot slide in. Eight pull-chains hang from the ceiling, scattered around the room (never beside their own gear). Each chain moves ONE gear ONE direction; the reverse is another chain. Pulling costs an action.

PULL-CHAINS (party — 1 action each)

Chain labels: gear + direction, e.g. "LW→" slides the lower-west gear toward the middle. Blue shading = squares reachable from the entrance; a chain someone can't reach can't be pulled.
Fritz has no control panel — but the C8 chain hangs beside his corner. He can pull it himself: sealing himself in, and revealing the way to the cradle.
Fritz and the golem are ordinary actors: both walk, both pull chains — nothing special. Verified tandem commute: escape via C8, B4, G2, C3, L2; on returning home, the golem's final pull of G6 (the crossing by its post) re-arms the maze — it seals itself into its cage.
